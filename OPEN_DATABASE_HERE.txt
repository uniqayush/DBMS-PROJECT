┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  HOW TO VIEW THE DATABASE:                                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  STEP 1: Download DB Browser for SQLite
          https://sqlitebrowser.org/
          (Free and easy to use!)

  STEP 2: Install and open the program

  STEP 3: Click "Open Database" button

  STEP 4: Navigate to this folder → server → lexilearn.db

  STEP 5: Click "Browse Data" tab

  STEP 6: Select "vocabulary" from the dropdown

  STEP 7: See all 1000+ words in a beautiful table! 🎉

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ALTERNATIVE - VIEW ONLINE (NO INSTALLATION):                │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  1. Go to: https://inloop.github.io/sqlite-viewer/
  2. Click "Choose File"
  3. Select: server/lexilearn.db
  4. View in your browser!
